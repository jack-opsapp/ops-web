# OPS Product Facts for the Journal

Version `ops-product-facts-2026-09-10-v1`. The only OPS product claims a journal post may make. Every line below is lifted from `ops-software-bible/14_FEATURE_POSITIONING.md` (last updated 2026-08-04), which cites the engineering chapter that proves each behaviour ships. Use the substance faithfully; rephrase for rhythm, never for scope. Anything about OPS that is not on this page does not go in a post.

How to use it in a weekly post: education earns its keep first. OPS appears at most once, late, as one soft line where it genuinely fits the move the reader is about to make — never as the lead, never as a pitch, never with a price.

## Brand facts

- Built by trades, for trades. The founder scaled a deck and railing business from $0 to $1.6M in four years, tried Jobber, ServiceTitan and Housecall Pro, found the crew would not use any of them, and built OPS.
- No training required: the crew opens it, sees their jobs, knows what to do.
- Free to start, then tiered by crew size; every tier has every feature. Never quote a price or a competitor comparison figure.

## Features that may be mentioned

**Inbox to pipeline** (source: `04_API_AND_INTEGRATION.md` § Email Pipeline Integration Routes; `10_JOB_LIFECYCLE_AND_DATA_RELATIONSHIPS.md` § Gmail Integration)
- Connect Gmail or Microsoft 365 and OPS pulls every quote request out of your inbox, sorts it, and drops it in pipeline.
- OPS classifies what's a lead, matches it to a client you already know, and queues it. Describe this as intelligent classification or by the behaviour; never "AI".
- Do not imply OPS replies to anyone on the operator's behalf.

**Pipeline** (source: `09_FINANCIAL_SYSTEM.md` § Pipeline / CRM System)
- Eight stages from first contact to signed contract: new lead, qualifying, quoting, quoted, follow-up, negotiation, won, lost.
- OPS moves leads forward as the work happens: logging a call, sending an estimate and an approval advance the stage. Drag manually when you need to.

**Project workspace** (source: `10_JOB_LIFECYCLE_AND_DATA_RELATIONSHIPS.md` § Design Philosophy)
- Photos, notes, tasks, estimates, invoices, site visits — all on one project. The crew sees the same thing on their phones.

**Estimates and the client portal** (source: `09_FINANCIAL_SYSTEM.md` § Estimates System; `11_CLIENT_PORTAL.md`)
- Send a branded estimate; the client opens it on their phone, picks the options, answers the questions you set, and signs.
- Questions attach to specific line items: pick a stain colour, pick a gate hardware finish, decide on corner caps.

**Invoices and payments** (source: `09_FINANCIAL_SYSTEM.md` § Invoices System, § Payments)
- One click converts the approved estimate to an invoice — deposit, milestones and balance already structured from the line items.
- Track what's paid and what's outstanding without a spreadsheet. Never claim a time-to-paid figure.

**Schedule** (source: `01_PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` § Calendar & Scheduling)
- Day, week, month, team and agenda views. Drag tasks where they need to be; the crew sees their day before they leave the driveway.
- Recurring routes generate themselves. Conflicts are flagged when you drag, so a crew member is not double-booked.

**Crew visibility** (source: `01_PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` § Team Management)
- See who's on which job and reassign in one tap, not five phone calls. The new person opens the app and the job is already there.
- Never describe it as tracking crew location.

**Field-first iOS app** (source: `01_PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` § Field-Specific Requirements; `04_API_AND_INTEGRATION.md` § SyncEngine)
- Jobs load in basements and dead zones because they were already on the device. Changes queue when signal drops and sync when it returns.
- Dark for sunlight, big buttons for gloves. iOS today; never imply Android ships.

**Photo capture and markup** (source: `01_PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` § Image Management)
- Take the photo on site, draw on it, add a note. It lives with the job, the crew sees it, and the client sees it in the portal when you choose.

**Site visits** (source: `10_JOB_LIFECYCLE_AND_DATA_RELATIONSHIPS.md` § SiteVisit)
- Start a site visit, capture photos, notes and measurements on site, link the lead, and the captured packet becomes the project. Nothing gets re-entered.

## Never mention

- AI email drafts or AI memory, a financial dashboard or cashflow forecast, turn-by-turn navigation as a headline, GPS tracking of crews, Android, any price, any competitor price, any customer result, any testimonial, any usage statistic about OPS.
- The only forward-looking line allowed: "We're working on supercharging your workflows with AI."
